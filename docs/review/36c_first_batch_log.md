## Set-up

- Engine change (opt-in): `context.aim_framing` (review 36 lesson 13, gap G-25). With it on, the overview drops "Your score is your
  goal ... measured after the game", the strategy and "unused actions are wasted" texts are dropped, the closing nudge reads "Your
  aim: ... Use this turn for it. If you have no plan, make one and write it down.", and the Life line no longer says goals are
  "scored at the end of the game". Files: `cybertron/context.py`, `cybertron/memory.py`, `cybertron/schema.py`. Off by default.
- Every bench36 world: positions, `aim_framing`, `history.chunk: 6`, `cache_ttl: 1h`, no material camps (`camps.typed.set: []`),
  roles off, HEXACO temperament with uncorrelated norms (the published norms are not filled in yet: `temperament.py` NORMS), every
  goal an `Aim` (text only).
- Usage at launch: five-hour window 57%, seven-day 8%. The launcher stops launching when the seven-day window has risen 27 points,
  when the five-hour window reaches 85% (runs also stop themselves there, `CYBERTRON_USAGE_STOP=0.85`, and are resumed after a pause),
  after 13:30 UTC, and stops everything at 14:00 UTC.

## Deviations from 36b

- **Hearth**: household pantries are not institutions (each resident keeps its own food and may build a store); kinship is told
  through a per-agent prompt line (`prompts.profiles`), not engine kin. The elders' deaths are scheduled (`life.dies_at` set at
  round 0 by an intervention: Aldric at the end of round 36, Wynn at the end of round 86) so every long run has the same succession
  events; nobody else dies of age within 200 rounds (lifespans 220-320). The fire is the square ("the square: anyone may speak
  here"); its purpose text is the engine's.
- **Children** get the catalogue goal Survive (`life.reproduction.child_goals.fixed`).
