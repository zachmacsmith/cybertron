# The calendar

## Summary

A round is a day; the year is compressed, its length a world setting. Daylight, and so the work a day allows, varies by season.

## Decided

- D4-13: a round is a day; the calendar is compressed (year length a world setting, about 16-60 days).
- D4-17: daylight varies by season (agents/work.md).

## Mechanics

- **Seasons**: spring, summer, autumn, winter, of equal length, set by the world (days a season); a world starts at a season and a
  day in it.
- **Cold**: winter is cold; a world may also mark cold days outside winter.
- **Daylight**: the work units of a day follow the season (agents/work.md).

## Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| year length | about 16-60 days | decided (D4-13; a world setting) |
| days a season | a quarter of the year | calibrate |

## What agents see

The season and the day of it ("autumn, day 3 of 6"); the cold in words.
