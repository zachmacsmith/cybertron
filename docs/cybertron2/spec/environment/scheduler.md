# The scheduler: external change

## Summary

External change (eras, cycles, incidents) is drawn from the seed by one central scheduler, the same in every arm of an experiment,
and kept apart from the internal consequences of what people do. It works on three time scales, each modulating the one below.

## Decided

- D4-4: external change is separate from internal consequences: eras, cycles and incidents drawn from the seed, the same in every
  arm, one central scheduler; depletion, exhaustion and collapse are the resources' own physics (environment/resources.md).
- D4-5: three time scales, each modulating the one below: eras and trends, cycles, incidents.
- D4-6: signs are kept separate from effects (information/signs.md).

## Mechanics

- **One scheduler** draws every external change from the world's seed, so two arms of an experiment meet the same weather, the same
  incidents on the same days (D4-4).
- **Eras and trends** (many years) shift the rates of **cycles** (the seasons' severity), which shift the rates of **incidents** (a
  day's events: a stray herd, a fire, a flood) (D4-5).
- **Incidents happen at a place** (D4-35) and make their changes through the engine's primitives (core/architecture.md).
- A world file may list incidents and signs by hand, for a given day and node.

## Data

Per incident: day, node, kind, and its parameters. Kinds of incident are data.
