# Later: goods, tools and methods not in the world yet

## Summary

A catalogue of options considered and **kept out of the first worlds**, so they are not lost and not built by accident. Nothing
here exists in the engine or in any spec. Each entry says what it would add, what it would need, its rough cost where known, and
why it waits. An entry moves into a spec file only when Zach decides it (DECISIONS.md). Owner: 37.2 (economy); entries for other
areas are noted and belong to their owners when they come in.

## Decided

- D2-10: clothing parked. D2-14: first worlds farm with the hoe and ox plough only. D2-19: a short metal ladder first. D2-23: traps,
  harness and saddle are in the tool set; armour, nets, boats and the seed drill wait.

## Tools and equipment

| Option | What it adds | Needs | Cost to make (units, 02j model) | Why it waits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **hide armour** | defence that is not used up (weapons are used up each fight); strength for the defender, which walls then multiply (D2-20) | hides; a leatherworker | 7.0 (4 hides, 3 units); about 3 years | fights may not need a defender's good; add if runs show it |
| metal armour | more defence; a smith's chokepoint | iron or bronze; a smith | iron-scale: tens of units | needs the metal ladder (D2-19) |
| **nets** | fishing at river and coast nodes | fibre (flax) | 5.0 | fibre is deferred (02g §1f) |
| **boats** | fishing; river transport (0.006 units a unit an edge, 02g §3) | timber, pitch; a boatwright; water | 16 | boats are deferred (02g §1f); river transport is a later era |
| seed drill | sows in rows with less seed and less work (D2-17's seed) | iron; a smith; an ox | not costed | a much later era |
| metal-jawed traps | traps that hold larger game | iron | not costed | needs iron |
