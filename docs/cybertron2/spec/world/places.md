# Places: nodes and sites

## Summary

A node is a village, a valley or a camp: the people there and the land they work. The places used every day are sites inside a
node, used without travelling. If you could go there and be back before nightfall, it is the same node.

## Decided

- D4-2: a node is a village, a valley or a camp; places used routinely are sites inside one node; an edge is a real trip.
- D4-11: node scale: if you could go there and be back before nightfall, it is the same node; a larger settlement is a cluster of
  nodes.
- D4-35: everything happens in a place (core/architecture.md).

## Mechanics

- **Presence is the node.** Who sees what, who can speak to whom in the talk step, and who can hand goods to whom are all "at the
  same node" (D4-2, D4-35).
- **Sites** inside a node are parcels of land (one cover each: forest, field, pasture, meadow, water, scrub, rock, built ground) and
  the buildings on built ground. Moving between sites costs no travel.
- **Clusters.** A larger settlement is several nodes joined by short edges, not one large node (D4-11).

## Data

Per node: name, its parcels, buildings and herds (each carrying the node). Per parcel: cover, size, neighbours, resources.
