# Signs

## Summary

Hidden change shows itself, if at all, as one in-world sentence: a sign. A sign has an audience, a lag and a reliability, and is
kept apart from the change it signals.

## Decided

- D4-6: signs: at most one in-world sentence, with an audience, a lag and a reliability, kept separate from the effects.
- D4-71: a slaughter shows itself as a bundle of signs on its parcel, that day and the next (its chances: D4-75).
- D4-74: the events and states of review 38.43 show themselves as bundles in the same pattern (below).
- D4-75: a sign row sets how plain it is, its sense, its conditions and its scope; the one noticing rule gives the chance.

## Mechanics

- **A sign** is a sentence about the world ("a white frost on the grass at dawn, weeks before its time") shown to those who could
  notice it: its audience (who is at the place, by sense and attention), after its lag, and true only with its reliability (D4-6).
- **Signs are local**: a sign happens at a place (D4-35) and travels further only as people carry it.
- The scheduler draws signs alongside the changes they signal (environment/scheduler.md).
- **A sign row** (D4-75): how plain the sign is (`own`), its sense, its conditions (`when`), its scope (`who`: the doer, the
  others, those inside) and optionally its reach (near: a close look at the spot; around: the spot and next door) and whether it is
  sensed in the dark; never a final chance. The chance is the noticing rule's (noticing.md). A bundle gives, where they fit, four
  kinds of sense: to the doer as they do it (felt, only what the result line does not say); to those nearby as it happens (by the
  rule's presence and carry); lingering traces on later days; and signs that exist only under conditions (snow, rain, frost, the
  dark, dogs kept, who is inside).
- **A slaughter** (D4-71, D4-75) records on its parcel the day's kills (the beast, its herd, who killed it) and shows for that day and
  the next (own `calibrate`; the chances are for an ordinary eye):

## Signs of people (D4-72)

A sign of a person shows their body (hunger, injury, age, pregnancy, what they carry or lead) and what they did; never an act or a
feeling they did not choose. Signs about those who live in a building apply only to buildings with beds. How to write signs:
docs/cybertron2/guides/senses.md.

## Data

Per sign: day, place, sense, text, salience, what it bears on.
