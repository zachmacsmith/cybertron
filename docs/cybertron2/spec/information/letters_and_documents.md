# Letters and documents

## Summary

Information crosses between nodes only by being carried. Rumour travels free with whoever goes; letters, and later papers that are
centralised and delivered, cost something to make and to carry. A document is a good: written on parchment, it has a place and can
be carried, stored, stolen and lost.

## Decided

- D4-9: transport and communication between nodes are delayed and carried: goods, letters and people cross only with someone.
- D2-8: wide reach costs more: rumour travels with merchants for free; letters, and later papers that are centralised and
  delivered, have costs and charges.
- D2-24: documents are first-world goods, written on parchment (from hides).
- D4-35: a document is somewhere: in a container at a place, or carried.

## Mechanics

- **Rumour:** what a traveller knows goes with it and is told where it arrives (speech.md); it costs nothing (D2-8).
- **A letter** is a document: made from parchment and writing, carried by someone to its reader, who learns its text on reading it;
  it crosses an edge only in a carrier's hands (D4-9, D2-8).
- **Documents** are items with a place, like any good (economy/goods.md): carried, stored, given, stolen, lost, burned (D2-24).

## Data

Per document: its text, author, the day and place written, where it is now.
