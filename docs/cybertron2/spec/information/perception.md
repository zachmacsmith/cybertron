# Perception: what an agent sees and knows

## Summary

What agents see, and how it changes, is the main design surface. An agent sees the node it is at and knows the rest only as it
has seen, been told or read. Absence is never computed for it, prices are never computed for it, and other villages are known by
the crafts of the lines born there.

## Decided

- H-7: perception matters most: what agents see, and how it changes, is the main design surface.
- D4-35: nothing that happens at one node can be seen at another, except as people, messages or goods carry it.
- D2-4: agents are never told someone is missing: a household saw the departure and remembers the stated plan; a death on the road
  is never announced.
- D2-29: prices are only what agents witnessed or were told; every settlement is witnessed by those present; no computed price is
  shown.
- D4-33: knowledge of other lines' crafts is awareness, not skill; a village's repute is the crafts of the lines born there.
- D4-6: hidden change shows only as signs (signs.md).

## Mechanics

- **Presence:** an agent sees the people, parcels, buildings, herds and events at its node that day, and what is said where it can
  hear (speech.md). Nothing else is shown live (D4-35).
- **Knowledge of elsewhere** is what it saw when last there, was told, or read, each with its age; its home while away is as it
  last saw it (D4-35).
- **Departures:** a departure and its stated plan are remembered by those who saw them; no line ever says someone has not been
  seen (D2-4).
- **Prices and settlements:** each settlement is seen by those present and remembered as a dated belief (D2-29).
- **Other villages:** known by repute, the crafts of the lines born there (D4-33).

## Data

Per agent: what it knows of places, people and things, with when and how it learned each.

## What agents see

The node in words, never as engine numbers; other places as they knew them, with how old the knowledge is.
