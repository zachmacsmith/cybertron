# Seeded populations

## Summary

A world starts as a society with a past: a full family tree of everyone, living and dead, back to the founders, with every kin tie
derived from it, and relationships, debts, holdings, standing, institutions and a short history, each written into each person's
knowledge from that person's own position.

## Decided

- D4-21: worlds are seeded as a society with a past, with a full family tree of the whole population (living and dead, back to the
  founders; every kin tie derived from it), relationships, debts, holdings, standing, institutions and a short history, written
  into each agent's knowledge from its own position.
- D4-33: a seeded person's knowledge of other lines' crafts is awareness, not skill; a village's repute is the crafts of the lines
  born there.
- D4-19: runs start with people of every age; no time skips.

## Mechanics

- **The tree** is simulated forward from founding couples to the start of the run; every kin tie a person knows is read from it
  (D4-21).
- **Each person's knowledge** of the past is written from where they stand: their kin, their household's history, the feuds and
  debts they are part of (D4-21).
- **Crafts**: a person knows its own line's crafts (and its birth line's, if married in); of other lines' crafts it is only aware
  (D4-33).
- **Repute** of a village, as others know it, is the crafts of the lines born there (D4-33).

## Data

The tree (every person, living and dead), the history, and each person's seeded memories and knowledge.
