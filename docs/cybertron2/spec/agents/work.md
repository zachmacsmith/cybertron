# Work in a day

## Summary

A day has a budget of work units; each act costs units by how long it takes; talking, reading and notes cost nothing, except that
speaking aloud takes a unit. The budget varies with the season's daylight, age and health. Units and costs are settled by
experiment.

## Decided

- D4-17: units a day and the action cost table are settled by experiment; daylight varies by season.
- D4-31: speaking aloud (to the town or in a building) takes one unit of the day's work (information/speech.md).
- D4-19: youths are agents (life/life_stages.md).

## Mechanics

- **Units a day** by season (daylight), fewer for a youth or an elder, fewer when hungry, starving or hurt.
- **Costs**: each act's cost is data on the act or its technique; free acts cost nothing.
- **Unused units are wasted**: nothing carries over.
