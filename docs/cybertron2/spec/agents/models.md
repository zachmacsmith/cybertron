# Models and casts

## Summary

Each agent is played by one model for its whole life, chosen when it becomes an agent. Heirs to a seat that is a pole of leverage
take that seat's model; the rest keep the cast balanced.

## Decided

- D4-20: one agent, one model, for life: chosen when it becomes an agent; heirs to pole seats take the seat's model; the rest keep
  the cast balanced.

## Mechanics

- The model is chosen once, when a person becomes an agent (a youth, life/life_stages.md), and never changes (D4-20).
- Casts follow scenario_design.md: the strongest model at every pole of leverage, a middle tier, and the bulk (D4-20 for heirs).
