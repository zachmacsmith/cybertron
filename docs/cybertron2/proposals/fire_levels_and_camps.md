# Levels of fire, and camps built out (flagged for later)

**Status:** deferred (flagged for later: OPEN L-46, L-47)
**Owner:** 37.4 (the night, fires and shelter in the engine); 37.2 owns fuel and shelter numbers

## Summary

Fire in three kinds:
- a small fire that cooks for a few;
- a big fire that burns more wood to cook for and warm more people;
- a hearth that burns least and keeps its heat into the next night.

Camps can be built out (more tents and lean-tos round one fire, a windbreak). Both wait for harsher weather, which is what makes a
poor fire or a thin camp fail.

## Why

Today:
- a house's fire burns a fixed heat for each hearth (D4-93);
- a camp's fire and an open fire are fixed (D4-49);
- a fire is shared by up to 4 in the open (D2-159).

There is no choice of how big a fire to make, and no gain from keeping a hearth warm.

## Open questions

1. The units of a fire's size: heat burned against people cooked for and warmed.
2. How long a hearth's warmth carries, and what relighting saves.
3. What a camp's extensions cost and give (beds, shelter state, store).
4. The extreme weather that makes the difference: frost, storms, deep snow.
