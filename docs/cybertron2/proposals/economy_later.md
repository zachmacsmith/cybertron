# Proposal: options kept for later (goods, tools, methods)

**Status:** deferred (23 Oct): kept out of the first worlds by D2-10, D2-14, D2-19 and D2-23. **Owner:** 37.2; entries for other
areas are noted and pass to their owners when they come in. **Affects:** spec/economy/goods.md, production.md, transport.md when an
entry is adopted.

## Summary

A catalogue of options considered and **kept out of the first worlds**, so they are not lost and not built by accident. Nothing
here exists in the engine or in the spec. Each entry says what it would add, what it would need, its rough cost where known, and
why it waits. An entry moves into the spec only when Zach decides it.

## Tools and equipment

| Option | What it adds | Needs | Cost to make (units, 02j model) | Why it waits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **hide armour** | defence that is not used up (weapons are used up each fight); strength for the defender, which walls then multiply (D2-20) | hides; a leatherworker | 7.0 (4 hides, 3 units); about 3 years | fights may not need a defender's good; add if runs show it |
| metal armour | more defence; a smith's chokepoint | iron or bronze; a smith | iron-scale: tens of units | needs the metal ladder (D2-19) |
| **nets** | fishing at river and coast nodes | fibre (flax) | 5.0 | fibre is deferred (02g §1f) |
| **boats** | fishing; river transport (0.006 units a unit an edge, 02g §3) | timber, pitch; a boatwright; water | 16 | boats are deferred (02g §1f); river transport is a later era |
| seed drill | sows in rows with less seed and less work (D2-17's seed) | iron; a smith; an ox | not costed | a much later era |
| metal-jawed traps | traps that hold larger game | iron | not costed | needs iron |

## Farming

| Option | What it adds | Needs | Why it waits |
|---|---|---|---|
| slash and burn | high returns on freshly burned forest for a few years, then move on (02l §4, "fresh land") | forest | D2-14 |
| irrigation (D2-67: later, with the weather; designed in farming.md) | much more food per plot, less blight | a river site; a crew to dig and keep ditches; an institution to share the water | D2-14 |
| heavy plough | breaks heavy clay soils; needs a team of oxen no household owns alone | iron share, many oxen | later era |
| new crops | land that was useless becomes fields (poor soil, cold valleys) | seed from beyond the frontier | 37.5's frontier |

## Transport (02g §3)

Later rungs: wagon (needs roads; pays above about 115 units a day on a route), river boat, rail. First worlds: foot, pack, pack
ox, handcart, ox cart.
