# Offers to children go to their parent

**Status:** retrospective, accepted: D4-55 (built 24 Oct at Zach's request; written down afterwards)
**Owner:** 37.4 (the talk step); 37.2 owns agreements

## Summary

An offer made to a child goes to the child's parent at the node instead: the mother, then the father, then the household's head or
another adult of it; the offerer is told so. With none of them here, the offer is refused.

## Why

- Day 3 of the region run (r1) crashed delivering an offer to Walter, a child: children take no part in the talk, so the offer had
  nowhere to go. A tell to a child was already refused. Zach: "Offers to children should go to their parent."

## Alternatives considered

- Refuse the offer (as first fixed).

## Open questions
